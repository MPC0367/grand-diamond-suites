01 Welcome

A quiet jewel set in the heart of Bangkok's most electric district.

Grand Diamond Suites is a four-star, all-suite hotel set between Platinum Fashion Mall and Pantip Plaza — a peaceful retreat wrapped in the hum of Bangkok's vibrant shopping district.

Every stay begins with space: well-maintained suites from 34 to 150 square metres, a welcoming atmosphere, and the kind of warm Thai hospitality that makes the city feel like it was arranged just for you.
