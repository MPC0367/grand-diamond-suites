Ploy Restaurant

Good to know

Is breakfast included?

The hotel's website does not say. Each rate on the hotel's booking page lists what it includes, so check before you confirm.

Dietary needs

No menu, allergen list or dietary certification is published. If you have an allergy or follow a particular diet, tell the hotel before you arrive.

Ask the hotel about dining

Evenings and room service

The hotel mentions dinner and room service, ordered to your suite, without publishing times for either. Ask the front desk about evening meals.
