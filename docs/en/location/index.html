From the airports

Bangkok has two airports on different rail lines. Journey times depend on traffic and time of day, so none are quoted here.

BKK

Suvarnabhumi Airport

By Airport Rail Link.

  1. Find the Airport Rail Link station on Level B1 of the terminal.
  2. Take the train towards Phaya Thai and leave at Ratchaprarop. It is the seventh of the line's eight stations, the stop before Phaya Thai.
  3. Ratchaprarop station is about 650 m from the hotel in a straight line. Walk from there, or take a taxi if you have luggage.

Check that the line is running to its usual timetable on the day you travel, especially for an early or late flight.

DMK

Don Mueang Airport

By train or airport bus. The Airport Rail Link does not serve Don Mueang.

  1. By train, take the SRT Red Line from Don Mueang station to Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal.
  2. Change to the MRT Blue Line at Bang Sue and ride to Chatuchak Park.
  3. Change to the BTS at Mo Chit and ride south to Ratchathewi, the BTS station the hotel names.

By bus instead: the city transit authority runs airport routes A1 and A2 from Don Mueang. A1 passes Mo Chit BTS station, where you can join the BTS as in step 3. A2 ends at Victory Monument, two BTS stops north of Ratchathewi.

The rail route uses three lines and two changes, so a taxi may be simpler with luggage. Bus routes and boarding points change. Confirm them at the airport on the day.

Routes checked against the airport, rail and bus operators' own published information on 8 October 2026. No fares or journey times are given because they vary.

Step-free arrival

The hotel has not published step-free routes from the street or the stations. Tell the hotel what you need before you travel and ask where to be dropped off.
