Suites

Find your space.

Six suite types across 176 suites. Start with what tells them apart, then open any one for its photographs and the hotel's full list.

Suite names are styles, not sizes: the Luxury Suite is smaller than the Junior Suite.

  • 1 bedroom

  • 1 bathroom
  • Up to 3 guests

Three beds and two bathrooms for staying together.

  • 45 sq m
  • King and two singles
  • 2 bathrooms
  • Up to 5 guests

Two bedrooms, a living room and a kitchenette.

  • 2 bedrooms

  • 1 bathroom
  • Up to 6 guests
