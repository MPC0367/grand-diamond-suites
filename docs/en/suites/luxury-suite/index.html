About this suite

The smallest and most compact of the six suite types. The hotel describes refined, modern decor in warm tones, a soft-top mattress for restful sleep and city views, with an en-suite bathroom and shower. The bedroom takes a king bed or twin beds. The hotel suggests it for corporate travellers and couples on a short business or leisure trip who want a practical room.

Good to know

Luxury is a room style here, not a size: this suite is smaller than the Junior Suite.

The booking page lists two rooms here: Luxury, for up to 2 guests, and Luxury Plus, for up to 4, both at 34 square metres. The hotel's website describes only the Luxury Suite, so ask the hotel how they differ.

The hotel's rooms page says each of its suites has a separate living area. Its list for the Luxury Suite does not mention one, and the photographs show the sofa in the same room as the bed. Ask the hotel if a separate living room matters to you.

The bathroom photographed has a bathtub as well as a shower. The hotel's list for this suite mentions a shower only, so ask the hotel if a bathtub matters to you.
