Green Hotel

The hotel states that it holds Green Hotel certification from the Department of Climate Change and Environment. The department's list of hotels that passed its assessment for 2025, posted on 8 January 2026, includes the hotel's Thai name, โรงแรมแกรนด์ไดมอนด์สวีท, at the Very Good (Silver) level, the second of three. The list gives names only, and no certificate number or end date is published.
