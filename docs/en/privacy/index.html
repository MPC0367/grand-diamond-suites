Privacy

Privacy

This page describes what this website does. It is written to be accurate about the site as built, and nothing more.

Who publishes this website

This website is an unofficial design concept published by O2 Design Studio. It is not published by Grand Diamond Suites Hotel, and the hotel is not responsible for it.

What this site does not have

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising tags and no cookies. The enquiry helper has nothing behind it: this site has no server of its own to receive what you type.

Four things do involve information about your visit, and each is described below: choices kept in your own browser, the request your browser makes to the web host for every page, the stay details you choose to pass to the hotel's booking page, and the satellite view of the map, if you switch it on.

What is kept in your browser

So that your choices survive a change of page or language, the site keeps your stay dates, number of adults, the ages of any children you add, the suite you last opened and your comparison choices in your browser's session storage. The site does not send them anywhere by itself. They leave your device only when you choose to continue to the booking page, as described in the next section.

The site also notes in session storage that its short opening animation has played, so that it plays when you arrive or reload a page, and not again on every page you open.

Browsers normally clear session storage when the tab is closed. A browser that restores closed tabs may bring the choices back with the tab.

If you switch language, that one choice is remembered in your browser's local storage, so the site's main address opens in the same language next time. It stays on your device until you clear your browser data.

Text you type into the enquiry helper is not stored. It exists only on the page, and is gone when you leave the page, reload it or change language. It goes nowhere unless you copy it or open it in your own email app.

What is sent when you go to the booking page

Choosing to check availability opens the hotel's official booking page in a new tab. The address of that page then carries the number of adults, the ages of any children you added and, if you chose dates, your arrival date and number of nights, so that the page can show them. For a party of more than 8 guests, only the dates are carried.

Nothing else is passed, and the link does not tell the hotel's website which page you came from. From there on, the hotel and its booking provider handle your information under their own policies.

The map and its satellite view

The street map on this site is drawn by the site itself from OpenStreetMap data and is served with the page. Moving it, zooming it and choosing a place on it ask nothing of any map service.

The map also has a satellite view. If you switch to it, your browser fetches the satellite images directly from Esri, a mapping company. Esri then receives what any website receives when it serves a picture: your IP address, your browser's details and which squares of the map you are looking at. This site does not tell it which page you are on. Nothing is asked of Esri unless you choose the satellite view, and the choice is not remembered: the map opens as the street map every time.

Other places this site links to

Links to maps, the hotel's social pages and its own website open those services, which apply their own policies. Fonts and photographs are served by this site itself.

Where the site is hosted

This preview is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. O2 Design Studio does not receive or use those records.
