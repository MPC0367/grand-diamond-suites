Questions

Before you book.

Where the hotel has published an answer, it is given here with its source. Where it has not, this page says so.

BookingSuites and guestsArrivingAt the hotel

Booking

Where do I complete my booking?

On the hotel's own booking page at granddiamondsuites.com. Prices, availability, payment and your confirmation all come from that page, which is run with the hotel's booking provider.

This website does not take bookings. It passes on your arrival date, number of nights, number of adults and the ages of any children, and nothing else.

Which details carry over to the booking page?

Your arrival date, the number of nights, the number of adults and the age of each child you add. This was checked against the booking page on 8 October 2026 and again on 12 October 2026.

Extra rooms and promo codes are added on the booking page itself. You also choose the suite there, so check that the room named on that page is the one you want.

Can I plan a long stay or a large party here?

When it was checked on 12 October 2026, the hotel's booking page took stays of up to 31 nights and up to 8 guests in one search. That is what the page showed for the requests tried, not a rule the hotel has stated.

For a longer stay or a larger party, the planner does not shorten the dates or drop a guest. It offers the booking page itself, where you can adjust the dates or add a room, or an enquiry with your dates and guests already filled in.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

They depend on the rate you choose. Each rate on the booking page shows its own conditions before you confirm, and those are the terms that apply.

This website does not hold or summarise them. See Booking terms for how the handover works.

Is a deposit taken at check-in?

The hotel has not published a deposit policy. Each rate on the booking page shows its payment conditions before you confirm; for anything collected on arrival, contact the hotel before you book.

Suites and guests

Which suite suits a family or a group?

The Family Suite has one king bed and two single beds, with two bathrooms. The Deluxe and Executive suites have two bedrooms with a king and two singles. The Penthouse has two bedrooms with king beds.

The hotel's booking page lists them for up to 5, 6, 6 and 8 guests. The comparison shows all six side by side.

Do children stay free?

The hotel's booking page states "Free stay for children under 7". How that applies to bedding and breakfast is not published.

Add each child and their age in the stay planner to see the price for your dates on the booking page, or contact the hotel first.

Can I add an extra bed or a cot?

The hotel has not published extra-bed or cot details, or any charge for them. Please contact the hotel before you book.

Are there smoking and non-smoking suites?

The booking page lists the Junior Suite as two separate rooms, smoking and non-smoking. Nothing is published for the other suite types, so ask the hotel if this matters to you.

Do all suites have a balcony?

The hotel lists outdoor space for two suites only: a private balcony for the Executive Suite and a private terrace for the Penthouse Suite. The other four are described with city view windows and no outdoor space is listed for them, so ask the hotel if a balcony matters to you.

Is the hotel suitable for wheelchair users?

The booking page lists a lift among the hotel's amenities. The hotel has not published step-free routes, door widths or adapted bathrooms, so please contact the hotel before booking and describe what you need.

Arriving

What are the check-in and check-out times?

The hotel's website pages do not state them. The summary on its booking page shows check-in from 14:00 and check-out until 12:00, read on 8 October 2026. The front desk is open 24 hours; contact the hotel if you need an early arrival or a late departure.

How do I reach the hotel from the airport?

From Suvarnabhumi Airport, the Airport Rail Link runs to Ratchaprarop, the station on that line that the hotel names.

The Airport Rail Link does not serve Don Mueang Airport. The location page sets out the options for both airports.

Is there parking?

The hotel lists on-site parking, and its booking page describes it as free indoor parking. A space cannot be reserved through this website, so if you are arriving by car, confirm with the hotel.

At the hotel

Is breakfast included?

The hotel's website does not say. Each rate on the booking page lists what it includes, so check the rate you choose.

Breakfast is an international buffet at Ploy Restaurant, served from 06:00 to 10:30. See Dining.

Does the restaurant serve dinner?

The published times cover breakfast, 06:00 to 10:30, and the à la carte menu, 11:00 to 15:00 with last order at 14:00. The hotel also mentions dinner and room service without giving times, so ask the hotel about evening meals.

When are the pool and fitness centre open?

Opening times are not published. Ask the front desk, which is open 24 hours.

Is Wi-Fi free?

Yes. The hotel lists free Wi-Fi throughout the property and free high-speed Wi-Fi in every suite.

Are pets allowed?

The hotel has not published a pet policy. Please contact the hotel before you book.

Can I hold a meeting at the hotel?

The hotel lists meeting facilities for business travellers. Room sizes, layouts and equipment are not published, so contact the hotel with your date and number of people.

Who made this website?

O2 Design Studio, as an unofficial design concept. It is not the hotel's website. Hotel details were read from the hotel's own website and booking page on 8 October 2026; the hotel remains the authority on all of them.

Still have a question?

The hotel is the authority on everything about your stay.
